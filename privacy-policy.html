oneSky Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Published by piyush-atrish

In short: oneSky does not collect, store or sell your personal information. Your location is used only on your phone, to work out which sky is above you, and is never sent anywhere. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

1. Location

If you allow it, oneSky reads your phone's approximate or precise location (Android permissions "approximate location" and "precise location", while the app is open) to calculate which stars, planets and satellites are above you right now.

  • Your location is used only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • It is kept in the app's memory while the app is running and is forgotten when the app is closed.
  • The app never asks for background location and does not track you.
  • You can say no. The app then lets you pick a city instead.

2. Data the app downloads

To show the International Space Station, oneSky downloads its public orbit data from CelesTrak (celestrak.org). This request contains no information about you or your location. As with any request on the internet, CelesTrak's servers can see the IP address of the network you are connected to. CelesTrak's own terms and privacy information apply to that service. oneSky keeps a copy of the orbit data on your phone, in the app's cache, for up to 7 days so it can work offline and avoid unnecessary requests.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account information. There are no accounts.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

4. Information stored on your device

The only information oneSky saves on your phone is the cached ISS orbit data described above. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

5. Children

oneSky does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to collect from or delete for a child.

6. Third-party content

The star, constellation and planet data in oneSky comes from the open sources credited on the app's About screen. They are used for display only and do not receive any information about you.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle data differently, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
